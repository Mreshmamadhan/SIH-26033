## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
FARM2FORK is an AI-powered digital agricultural marketplace that directly connects farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), consumers, retailers and bulk buyers, eliminating unnecessary intermediaries.

The platform uses Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning and GPS technology to improve agricultural marketing, provide fair prices, predict demand and optimize transportation.

Key Features:

Direct farmer-to-consumer marketplace.

AI-based fair price prediction.

Machine learning-based demand forecasting.

GPS-based logistics and route optimization.

Bulk ordering for hotels, supermarkets and retailers.

Multilingual support for rural farmers.

Secure digital payments and order tracking.

Farmer and product verification.

2. Detailed Explanation of the Proposed Solution
The platform provides a common digital marketplace where farmers can sell agricultural products directly to buyers.

Working Process:

Farmer Registration: Farmers register and provide their location and agricultural details.

Product Listing: Farmers upload crop names, quantities, prices and harvest dates.

AI Price Prediction: AI analyzes market prices, demand and supply to suggest fair selling prices.

Buyer Connection: Consumers and bulk buyers can search, compare and purchase products.

Smart Group Selling: Nearby farmers combine their products to fulfil bulk orders.

Smart Logistics: GPS and route optimization identify efficient delivery routes.

Digital Payment: Buyers make secure payments, and farmers receive their earnings.

Feedback System: Buyers provide ratings and reviews to improve service quality.

Innovation and Uniqueness:

Eliminates unnecessary intermediaries through direct transactions.

Uses AI to recommend fair agricultural prices.

Combines produce from multiple farmers for bulk orders.

Predicts demand to reduce overproduction and wastage.

Optimizes delivery routes to minimize transportation expenses.

Provides voice-assisted and regional-language support.



## Technical Approach
AI Implementation
1. AI-Based Price Prediction

Uses historical market prices, crop availability, demand and transportation expenses to recommend suitable selling prices.

2. Demand Forecasting

Predicts future demand using previous sales, seasonal patterns, weather information and local consumption trends.

3. Route Optimization

Identifies nearby farmers and buyers, combines compatible orders and calculates efficient delivery routes to reduce fuel consumption and delivery costs.
